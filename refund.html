Refund Policy

We want you to be confident working with us. This policy explains when refunds apply across our courses, services, and digital products.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1Overview

Because our work involves personalised services and custom digital products, refund eligibility depends on the type of service and how much work has begun. Please read the section relevant to you.

2Courses & Training

If you cancel before a batch begins, you may request a refund of fees paid, minus any non-refundable registration charges. Once classes have started, fees are generally non-refundable, though we’re happy to discuss rescheduling or credit where possible.

3Web & Marketing Services

Project work is quoted and delivered in stages. Advance payments cover work already scheduled and resources committed. Once a stage of work has begun, amounts for that stage are non-refundable. Unstarted milestones may be refundable on request.

4Digital Invitations

Digital invitations are custom-made products. Once design work or delivery has started, the order is generally non-refundable. If there is an issue with your order, contact us promptly and we’ll work to make it right with revisions or a suitable resolution.

5How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us with your name, order/enrolment details or payment reference, and the reason for your request. If you were charged but have no confirmed Order ID, please include the payment reference:

  • Email: contact@allbeesolutions.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +91 89036 07506

6Processing Time

Approved online-payment refunds are initiated to the original payment method. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment method, gateway and bank. Contact us with your payment reference if you need a status update.

7Contact Us

Have a question about a refund? We’re here to help — reach out any time during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM).
