FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast will I receive my invitation?
Most invitations are delivered within 2–4 days of receiving your details. Premium and Elite packages get priority turnaround, often faster.
Do I get revisions?
Yes. Premium includes revisions, and Elite includes unlimited revisions — we refine it until you're completely happy.
Is hosting included for website invitations?
Yes — your invitation website is hosted for you and stays live through your event at no extra cost. You just share the link.
Can I use my own domain?
A custom domain (e.g. yourname.com) is available on the Elite package. On other packages you get a clean, shareable AllBee link.
Can I share it on WhatsApp?
Absolutely — every invitation is a single link that previews beautifully on WhatsApp, with QR-code sharing too.
Is there a guest limit?
No — invite unlimited guests. Live RSVP, countdown and guest wishes are built in at no extra charge.
How do I get support?
We're on WhatsApp throughout — message us anytime at +91 89036 07506 and our team will help with details, edits or delivery.
