FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see a live demo before ordering?
Yes — click “View Demo” on any template to preview a sample invitation with event details, QR code and RSVP, just as your guests would see it.
Can the designs be customised for my event?
Absolutely. Every template can be personalised with your names, dates, venue, photos, colours and language — Tamil, English or Urdu.
How will I receive my invitation?
PDF and image invitations are delivered ready to share on WhatsApp. Website invitations come as a private link you can send to any guest.
Do website invitations support RSVP?
Yes. Guests can RSVP directly from your invitation website, and you can track responses and headcounts in one place.
How long does delivery take?
PDF invitations are typically delivered within 24 hours. Website and premium invitations are completed quickly based on your customisation.
How do I place an order?
Click “Order Now” on any design. Choose your package, enter your details, pay securely, then send your content for personalisation and approval.
